**greedy IS a faithful, generous proxy for the AQORN/Jin placement-time realization-choice paradigm, adapted to a constrained map (defensible from code, not rhetoric).** `GreedyCommit._greedy_pick` = per-op argmin of total 2Q chip-distance under a SabreLayout-refined placement, inside a layout⇄commit refinement loop. That is term-for-term AQORN's "select the cheapest realization given qubit placement," with chip-distance substituting their negligible-move all-to-all cost. It is generous (gets refined placement + full per-op argmin + iteration = the best a placement-time method can do on a constrained map), so the prism win over it is the value of the routing↔realization COUPLING (defer the choice into the routing min), which the frozen-pick ablation isolates — not a smarter-router-vs-strawman gap.

**Incomparability map (state explicitly in related work; each has a one-line structural reason):** AQORN/Jin = all-to-all / negligible-move, NO routing (their realization choice gets nothing from routing); TeleSABRE/dSABRE = MULTI-CORE (teleport candidate boundary-forced, intra-core gates never see it); RTG = single-device but BARE 1:1 qubits + spare-qubit teleport chains + post-pass = a connectivity-EXTENSION method, not a realization-CHOICE method. The constrained-map / encoded / in-loop-realization-min cell is empty; greedy is the faithful in-cell baseline.

**RTG (arXiv:2502.04138) is NOT a portable head-to-head (verified 2026-06-09):** no public code; operates on bare qubits (no encoding, no realization set); REQUIRES spare/unused physical qubits to build 1D teleport chains (e.g. 20 logical on 50 physical → 30 auxiliary), which our packed encoded grid (dual-rail = 2 wires/logical, map fitted to ~n logical) does not have → RTG starves in our exact regime (itself a finding); post-pass, not in-loop. **The runnable substitute** = reimplement an RTG-style two-pass selector INSIDE our IR (route → measure per-op pressure → reselect realizations → reroute) as Config D in `pick_ablation.py --with-d` (reuses `PinRealizations`; no RTG source / spare qubits needed).

**CONFIG D = RUN, RESULT = THE POSITIVE (2026-06-10, `configd_results.md`):** D starts from greedy's placement-time picks (steelman), routes pinned (prism's router, no in-loop min), reselects argmin chip-distance under the achieved layout, reroutes, iterates. n=8, 3 seeds, 6 circuits: **C ≤ D on depth in ALL 18 cells, C < D strictly on every circuit with realization freedom (qft/qaoa/modular_adder/hhl), C == D exactly where picks are forced (ghz/vqe_su2, conv@0); D NEVER beats C.** Totals: A(greedy) 648/439, D(two-pass) 533/415, C(prism) 457/302 (depth/swaps). MECHANISM (the two-number story): D recovers 60% of the greedy→prism DEPTH gap but only 18% of the SWAP gap — the routing-aware CHOICE that cuts swaps (94% pick-share in the frozen-pick ablation) is exactly what a frozen-then-reroute structurally cannot capture (choosing op i's realization moves where op j sits when routed = the placement-selection coupling). NOT an iteration-budget artifact: D@2→D@8 improves but asymptotes ABOVE C and never converges (oscillates; qaoa C=14/6 vs D@8=16/12, hhl C=48/52 vs D@8=52/55, conv hits cap at every budget). ⇒ the in-loop coupling beats the two-pass RTG/AQORN paradigm on the constrained map, on the same benchmark = the prior-work head-to-head a reviewer demands, landed as a positive. trivial/logical baselines = the "obvious approach" floor.
